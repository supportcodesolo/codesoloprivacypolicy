Welcome to CodeSolo (“CodeSolo”, “we”, “us” or “our”), a coding-learning application built and operated from India by its founders, Narasimha and Venu. This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, who we share it with, and the rights and choices you have.

By creating an account or using CodeSolo, you confirm that you have read this Policy and consent to the practices described in it. Please also read our Terms and Conditions. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the App.

1Information We Collect

a) Information you provide

  • Your name, email address and profile picture, received when you sign in with your Google account
  • Username, bio and country that you choose to add to your profile
  • Posts, comments, replies, doubts and code projects you create (public or private)
  • Messages and photos you send to other users
  • Questions and code you submit to the in-app AI assistant
  • Reports you file against other users, and the users you choose to block
  • Emails and other communications you send to us

b) Information generated by your use of the App

  • Learning progress, completed pages, quiz and daily-challenge results, XP, level, lives and streak
  • Challenge history against other users, win and loss records, and leaderboard position
  • Who you follow and who follows you
  • Likes, bookmarks, views and comments on posts and code projects
  • “Last seen” and online status (you can turn this off in Settings)
  • Notification preferences, daily AI usage counts and daily reward-claim counts
  • Premium subscription status, plan type and expiry date, where you have purchased a plan

c) Technical information

  • Device type, operating system version, and a push-notification token so that we can send you alerts
  • Device time zone, used to deliver reminders at a suitable local time
  • Advertising identifier, IP address and ad-interaction data, collected by our advertising partners (see Section 7)
  • Basic diagnostic and usage information, such as crash reports and which features are used

What we do not collectWe do not collect your phone number, precise location, contacts, microphone recordings, bank details or payment card numbers. Purchases are processed by Google Play, which does not share your card details with us.

2How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in
  • To save and display your learning progress, XP, lives, streaks, certificates and rankings
  • To run challenges, quizzes, leaderboards, the community feed, doubts and code sharing
  • To deliver messages between users and show online status (if enabled)
  • To send notifications such as messages, follows, comments, challenges, streak reminders and daily challenges
  • To power the AI assistant and the online code runner
  • To show advertisements and allow you to earn rewards by watching them
  • To manage Premium subscriptions and unlock the related benefits
  • To detect spam, abusive language, cheating and policy violations, and to act on user reports
  • To fix bugs, keep the App secure and improve our features
  • To comply with applicable law and respond to lawful requests

No sale of dataWe do not sell your personal information.

3Consent and Legal Basis

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you create an account and accept this Policy, and where processing is necessary to provide the App, to comply with the law, or for other lawful purposes permitted under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law.

You may withdraw your consent at any time, as described in Section 16. Withdrawing consent may mean that we can no longer provide some or all features of the App to you.

4What Other Users Can See

CodeSolo is a social learning App. Depending on the feature, other users can see:

  • Your public profile: username, profile picture, bio, country, level, XP, followers and following, completed-course progress, challenge history and public code projects
  • Leaderboards: username, picture, level, XP and country
  • Posts, comments, doubts and public projects: your username and picture together with what you post
  • Online status: whether you were recently active (you can switch this off in Settings)

Your email address is shown only to you. Private code projects are visible only to you.

ImportantAnything you post publicly can be seen, copied or screenshotted by others. Do not post personal contact details, passwords or other sensitive information.

5Messages and Photos

  • Direct messages are stored only for a limited time and are automatically deleted from our systems after that period.
  • Photos sent in chats are compressed and disappear automatically after about one hour.
  • A recipient can still save a photo or take a screenshot of a conversation before it disappears. We cannot prevent this.
  • Messages are filtered by automated checks and may be reviewed by us if they are reported.
  • You can delete a message for yourself, or for everyone if you sent it.
  • Until two users follow each other, only a small number of messages can be sent between them. A daily message limit also applies.

Please noteMessaging in CodeSolo is not end-to-end encrypted and is not designed for confidential communication. Do not share sensitive information in chats.

6AI Assistant and Online Code Runner

AI assistant

When you use the in-app AI helper, the question you type, recent conversation turns, and the code and program output shown in your editor are sent to a third-party AI service in order to generate a reply. Do not enter passwords, personal data or confidential information in your code or questions. AI answers may be inaccurate, so always verify them. Daily usage limits apply.

Online code runner

When you run code in the Java, C, C++, SQL and other supported compilers, your code and any input you provide are sent to our servers and processed by an execution service so that we can return the output. Do not run code that contains personal or secret information. Web previews (HTML, CSS and JavaScript) and some other previews run on your device.

7Advertising

The free version of CodeSolo is supported by advertisements, including banner, full-screen and optional rewarded video ads. These ads are provided by third-party advertising partners. Where available, we request non-personalised ads.

  • Advertising partners may collect your device advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad-interaction data in order to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • This data is handled under the advertising partners’ own privacy policies, and we do not control it.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone’s Settings under Privacy or Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. Rewards such as extra lives, bonus XP, AI uses or a certificate download are granted only after you watch the ad.
  • Premium users may see fewer or no ads, as described in the App.

8Premium Subscriptions and Payments

If you purchase a Premium plan, the payment is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card number, bank details or other payment credentials. We receive only the information needed to confirm and manage your subscription, such as the plan type, status and expiry date.

Billing, renewal, cancellation and refunds are governed by Google Play’s terms and policies, as well as our Terms and Conditions.

9Notifications

With your permission, we send push notifications about messages, comments, likes, follows, challenges, streaks and daily challenges, as well as occasional in-app announcements. You can turn each type on or off in Settings → Notifications, or disable notifications entirely in your phone’s settings.

CodeSolo is intended for users aged 13 and above and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, and we do not show targeted advertising to children.

17Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the App. If you cannot access the App, email supportcodesolo@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject “Delete my account”.

  • Your profile, progress, XP, posts, comments, projects, follows and messages are permanently deleted.
  • Deletion is irreversible, and virtual items such as XP, lives and certificates are lost.
  • Some data may remain briefly in backups before being overwritten, and minimal records may be kept where the law requires.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Please cancel any active subscription in the Google Play Store.

18Safety, Scams and Links

Beware of scamsOther users may send suspicious links or ask for money, one-time codes, passwords or personal details. Do not click, do not share, do not pay. Use Report and Block. CodeSolo is not responsible for losses caused by interactions with other users or external websites.

We apply automated filters against links, spam and abusive language, and we act on reports. Users who receive repeated valid reports may be suspended or permanently banned.

If you find a security issue, please report it responsibly to supportcodesolo@gmail.com before sharing it publicly. Unauthorised access or hacking attempts are offences under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and will be pursued legally.
