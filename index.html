1Information We Collect

a) Information you give us

  • Your name, email address and profile picture, received when you sign in with your Google account
  • Username, bio and country that you choose to add to your profile
  • Posts, comments, replies, doubt questions and code projects you create (public or private)
  • Messages and photos you send to other users
  • Questions and code you submit to the in-app AI assistant
  • Reports you file against other users, and users you choose to block
  • Messages you send us by email

b) Information generated by your use of the app

  • Learning progress, completed pages, quiz and daily-challenge results, XP, level, lives and streak
  • Challenge history against other users, win/loss records and leaderboard position
  • Who you follow and who follows you
  • Likes, bookmarks and views on posts and code projects
  • "Last seen" / online status (you can turn this off in Settings)
  • Your notification preferences, daily AI usage counts and daily reward-claim counts

c) Technical information

  • Device type, operating system, and a push-notification token so we can send you alerts
  • Advertising identifier and related device/ad-interaction data, collected by our advertising partners (see Section 6)
  • Basic diagnostic and usage information (for example crashes or which features are used)

ℹ️ We do not collect your phone number, precise location, contacts, microphone or camera data, bank details or card numbers.

2How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in
  • To save and show your learning progress, XP, lives, streaks, certificates and rankings
  • To run challenges, quizzes, leaderboards, the community feed, doubts and code sharing
  • To deliver messages between users and show you who is online (if enabled)
  • To send notifications such as messages, follows, comments, challenges, streak reminders and daily challenges
  • To power the AI assistant and the online code runner
  • To show advertisements and let you earn rewards by watching them
  • To detect spam, bad words, abuse, cheating and policy violations, and to act on user reports
  • To fix bugs, keep the app secure and improve features
  • To comply with applicable law and respond to lawful requests

✅ We do not sell your personal information.

3What Other Users Can See

CodeSolo is a social learning app. Depending on the feature, other users can see:

  • Your public profile: username, profile picture, bio, country, level, XP, followers/following, completed-course progress, challenge history and public code projects
  • Leaderboards: username, picture, level, XP and country
  • Posts, comments, doubts and public projects: your username and picture along with what you post
  • Online status: whether you were recently active (can be switched off in Settings → Show Online Status)

Your email address is shown only to you. Private code projects are visible only to you.

⚠️ Anything you post publicly can be seen, copied or screenshotted by others. Do not post personal contact details, passwords or sensitive information.

4Messages & Photos

  • Direct messages are stored only for a limited time and are automatically deleted from our systems after that period.
  • Photos sent in chats are compressed and disappear automatically after 1 hour.
  • A recipient can still save a photo or screenshot a conversation before it disappears. We cannot prevent this.
  • Messages may contain links or content filtered by our automated checks. Messages may be reviewed by us if they are reported.
  • You can delete a message for yourself, or for everyone if you sent it.
  • Until two users follow each other, only a small number of messages can be sent between them. A daily message limit also applies.

ℹ️ CodeSolo messaging is not designed for confidential communication. Do not share sensitive information in chats.

5AI Assistant & Online Code Runner

AI assistant

When you use the in-app AI helper, the question you type, recent conversation turns, and the code (and program output) shown in your editor are sent to a third-party AI service to generate a reply. Do not put passwords, personal data or confidential information in your code or questions. AI answers may be inaccurate — always double-check them. Daily usage limits apply.

Online code runner

When you press RUN in Java, C, C++ or SQL compilers (and other supported languages), your code and any input you provide are sent to our servers and processed by an execution service so we can return the output. Do not run code that contains personal or secret information. Web (HTML/CSS/JS) and Python previews run on your device.

6Advertising

CodeSolo is free to use and is supported by advertisements, including banner, full-screen and optional "watch a video to earn a reward" ads. These ads are provided by third-party advertising partners. We request non-personalised ads where available.

  • Ad partners may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud.
  • This data is handled under the ad partners' own privacy policies; we do not control it.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy / Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. Rewards (extra lives, bonus XP, AI chances) are granted only after you watch the ad.

7Notifications

With your permission, we send push notifications (messages, comments, likes, follows, challenges, streak and daily-challenge reminders) and occasional in-app announcements. You can turn each type on or off in Settings → Notifications, or disable notifications entirely in your phone's settings.

9Sharing of Information

We share information only in these situations:

  • Service providers that help us run the app — such as sign-in, data storage and hosting, push notifications, AI processing, code execution and analytics. They may process your data only to provide services to us.
  • Advertising partners as described in Section 6.
  • Other users as described in Section 3 and Section 4.
  • Legal and safety reasons — to comply with law, court orders or government requests, to enforce our Terms, or to protect users and the public from fraud, abuse or harm.
  • Business changes — if CodeSolo is transferred, merged or restructured, your information may move to the new owner under this Policy.

We never sell your personal data or give your email address to advertisers.

10Data Retention

  • Account data and progress: kept while your account is active.
  • Chat messages and chat photos: deleted automatically after a short period (photos after 1 hour).
  • Doubt posts: removed automatically about 10 hours after posting.
  • Notifications and temporary data: kept only as long as needed to run the feature.
  • Reports and moderation records: kept as long as needed to keep the community safe.
  • After account deletion: your data is deleted (see Section 14), except minimal records we must keep for legal compliance or fraud prevention.

11Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures — including encrypted connections and access controls — to protect your information. However, no app or internet service can be 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

⚠️ Keep your Google account secure. Never share one-time codes or passwords with anyone claiming to be from CodeSolo. We will never ask for them.

12Children's Privacy

CodeSolo is intended for users aged 13 and above.

👨‍👩‍👧 Users under 18 should use the app with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian. Children under 13 must not use CodeSolo without verifiable parental consent and supervision; the parent or guardian is responsible for the account.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without parental consent, and we do not show targeted advertising to children. If you believe a child has used the app without consent, email us and we will delete the account promptly.

13Your Rights & Choices

In line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules made under them, you may:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct or update inaccurate data (most details can be edited in the app under Edit Profile)
  • Request erasure of your data / delete your account
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (withdrawing consent may mean you can no longer use the app)
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity
  • Raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer (Section 18)

In-app controls: Show Online Status, Notification preferences, Blocked Users, Edit Profile, public/private project visibility, and Delete Account.

To use any right not available inside the app, email supportcodesolo@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity first.

14Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app. If you cannot access the app, email supportcodesolo@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject "Delete my account".

  • Your profile, progress, XP, posts, comments, projects, follows and messages are permanently deleted.
  • Deletion is irreversible and virtual items (XP, lives, certificates) are lost.
  • Some data may remain briefly in backups before being overwritten, and minimal records may be kept where the law requires.

15Safety, Scams & Links

🚫 Beware of scams. Other users may send suspicious links or ask for money, OTPs, passwords or personal details. Do not click, do not share, do not pay. Use Report and Block. CodeSolo is not responsible for losses caused by interactions with other users or external websites.

We apply automated filters against links, spam and abusive language and act on reports. Users who receive repeated valid reports may be suspended or permanently banned.

✅ Found a security bug? Please report it responsibly to supportcodesolo@gmail.com before sharing it publicly. Unauthorised access or hacking attempts are offences under the IT Act, 2000 and will be pursued legally.
